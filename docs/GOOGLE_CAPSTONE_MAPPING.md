# Google Data Analytics certificate — capstone alignment

The author completed the **Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate**, an eight-course Coursera programme in 2025, including the Google Data Analytics capstone. This repository is a **new, independent post-course portfolio case study** — not a submission that Google or Coursera assessed.

Official reference: [Google Data Analytics Capstone: Complete a Case Study](https://www.coursera.org/learn/google-data-analytics-capstone)

| Phase | Evidence available here |
|---|---|
| Ask | Business question about historical monthly gross sales, high-value markets/products and data-quality risks |
| Prepare | Source and data licence citation, business definitions, historical/population caveats and raw Excel downloader |
| Process | Type and date checks, duplicate handling, missing customer tracking and explicit cancellation classification |
| Analyze | Monthly trends, country/product rankings, orders/AOV, independent SQLite reconciliation and optional base-R check |
| Share | Reproducible PNG charts, audit JSON and CSV reports, explanatory README and visual synthetic preview |
| Act | Reporting quality assurance and targeted follow-up recommendations; no invented commercial impact |

The Google course included **Data Analysis with R Programming**. Python automation and CI are **additional independent skills**, not a claim of an original course requirement.

## Reviewer questions to consider

1. Is gross sales clearly separated from net revenue and refunds?
2. Were exact duplicate rows removed consistently across calculations?
3. Does a distinct invoice represent a single order for this source?
4. Are missing customer identifiers handled without false customer merging?
5. Do the independent SQL checks agree with Pandas results?
6. Which proposed business decisions are unsupported by the dataset?
